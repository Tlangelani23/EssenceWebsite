Who We Are

A Passion for Perfume

Essence of the Middle East is a premier perfume destination located in the vibrant heart of Cape Town City. We specialise in bringing the rich, captivating world of Middle Eastern fragrances to South African shores.

Our collection features well-known luxury brands alongside exclusive fragrances that are difficult to find elsewhere. From the deep, woody notes of authentic Arabian oud to delicate floral compositions, our selection caters to every taste and occasion.

What truly sets us apart is our personalised scent creation service. Our experienced perfumers work with you one-on-one to craft a bespoke fragrance that is uniquely yours — a signature scent that tells your personal story.
